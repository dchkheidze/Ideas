Memos: `board/offers.md`, `board/monopoly.md`, `board/product.md`.

## Risks more than one member raised
1. **Thin supply at the first moment.** 1 to 4 informal crews, no written agreement. An owner who gets one bid or none has no real choice. Raised by all three.
2. **Money-flow seam undecided.** Manual stage payments vs platform-held money. Staged release is the strongest promise in the offer, but it isn't built yet. Product and Offers both flag the late decision as the risk.
3. **Disintermediation.** Owner and crew can sign the next job directly. Only platform-held money or a real reason to stay after sign-off prevents this (Monopoly).
4. **Acquisition cost unknown.** Nothing says what the first 20 to 100 owners cost, so the fee may never cover acquisition (Monopoly, Offers).
5. **"Verified" is undefined.** Nothing says who checks crews, against what, or how long it takes (Offers, Product).

## Where they disagree
- Product sees the brief as the best decision in the file and the cleanest first test. Offers wants a demand test before most of the budget is spent. Monopoly wants a crew bypass clause before anything else.

## Conditions checklist (merged)
Focus
- [ ] Name one Tbilisi district and count the owners and crews there. *(Monopoly)*
- [ ] Pilot scope in writing: flat renovation, one city, one owner type, no new houses. *(Product)*

Money
- [ ] Choose the money-flow branch with the lawyer before building. *(all three)*

Supply
- [ ] Written pilot letters or agreements with crews: bid terms, stage sign-off, disputes, no-bypass clause. *(all three)*
- [ ] Define "verified" in writing, with who checks and the proof an owner sees. *(Offers, Product)*
- [ ] Agree a minimum bid count (3 suggested) before any owner is promised a choice. *(Product)*

Demand and cost
- [ ] Demand test with real owners before most of the budget: pass mark set in advance (posts, replies, deposit intent). *(Offers)*
- [ ] Measure cost per owner in a small paid test; the founder sets the ceiling. *(Monopoly, Offers)*
- [ ] Test the owner's total cost against hiring a crew directly; choose the fee after that test. *(Offers)*

Product
- [ ] Test the standard brief with 5 Tbilisi owners and 3 crews; keep only the fields crews need to bid. *(Product)*
- [ ] Write the full flow once and name the owner of every handoff. *(Product)*

## The strongest version the board can see
A Tbilisi flat-renovation pilot: one district, one owner type, one standard brief, three or more written crew commitments, and staged payment chosen with a lawyer. The first deliverable is proof that owners post briefs and crews bid on them, before any platform is built. The version that fails is the one that promises verified crews and safe payment before any crew has finished a job through the process.
