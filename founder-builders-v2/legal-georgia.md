## B. Construction works and contractor licensing

### B1. What the construction law says

**Law of Georgia on Construction Activities** (No. 577, 27 October 2000). Official text: https://matsne.gov.ge/en/document/view/17338

- **Art. 3(h)** defines a *contractor* as a legal entity or sole entrepreneur that carries out pre-project, design, building, assembly and fitting works and "has received a licence for design and construction works according to the established procedure". It is responsible for its own works and its subcontractors' works.
- **Art. 3(k)** defines a *licence* as a special permit of the state body that authorises its holder to carry out planning, design and construction works under set conditions.
- **Art. 3(l)** names the supervising body as the legal entity under public law, the **Technical and Construction Supervisory Agency**, plus regional and municipal bodies within their remit.
- **Art. 6(2)** says the client must select a duly qualified contractor, ensure construction design expertise, obtain the construction commencement permit from the authorised bodies, and finance the works.
- **Art. 6(3)** says construction products must have the certificates required by law.
- **Art. 7** says the economic and financial relations between participants are set by contract.
- **Art. 8** gives the Ministry of Economy and Sustainable Development the state policy role for construction.
- **Art. 13** says courts decide disputes between participants.

**Version caveat:** the page we read is the 24/09/2013 consolidated publication (publication 7). The matsne page lists a later consolidated publication of 15/07/2020 (publication 9). Read the latest version before relying on any article.

### B2. Permits for apartment renovation

**Code of Spatial Planning, Architectural and Construction Activities of Georgia (2019).** Official text: https://matsne.gov.ge/en/document/view/4276845 . Resolution No. 255 (2019) on permits: https://matsne.gov.ge/document/view/4578072?publication=0

A lawyer-written guide (JUST Advisors, 10 June 2025) says a permit is needed for:
- redevelopment that changes load-bearing walls;
- relocating or enlarging bathrooms or kitchens where it affects wet areas;
- changing the room's use;
- changing the facade (windows, doors, balconies, facade air-conditioners);
- increasing total area or merging rooms;
- consolidating apartments in the cadastre.

No permit, per the same guide, for: removing or installing non-load-bearing partitions; finishing (paint, tile, wallpaper); replacing plumbing without moving communications; changing interior doors and windows (not the front door).

Source: https://en.justadvisors.ge/stroitelstvo-remont-pereplanirovka-gruzii (a law firm blog, not the law). A builder's blog says "not all renovations require a permit" (https://www.gamiremonte.ge/en/blog/renovation-permits-georgia).

**Who issues:** the municipality. For Tbilisi, the Tbilisi Architecture Service (under the Tbilisi Mayor's Office) is named in one guide (https://www.enterprisegeorgia.gov.ge/uploads/files/publications/5b4dd76b9c261-7.pdf is older and not checked). The JUST Advisors guide says the Tbilisi City Hall / mayor's office issues the permit.

**Review period (conflict):** 20 working days (JUST Advisors, if no extra approvals); 20–45 working days (Gamiremonte); 60 days (a search summary of the Law on Licences and Permits). **Conflict, verify.** Law on Licences and Permits: https://matsne.gov.ge/en/document/view/26824

**Cultural heritage:** old buildings in central Tbilisi may be protected. Work on protected buildings needs an approval from the national cultural heritage agency. Fines reach GEL 25,000 and more. Source: JUST Advisors guide above; Law No. 2277 on Cultural Heritage: https://matsne.gov.ge/document/view/21076?publication=22

### B3. Is a contractor licence needed for apartment renovation?

- The 2000 law's definitions (Art. 3(h), (k)) require a licence for "design and construction works" for a *contractor*. That is the core rule we could verify.
- We could **not** verify the current licensing regime, which licence class covers apartment interior renovation, the threshold below which a licence is not needed, or the licensing body and fee. Search results for "Georgia contractor licence" returned US state licensing pages, which do not apply.
- A lawyer-written guide says a person doing construction work for individuals may work as a sole entrepreneur with the small-business tax status (1% on turnover up to 500,000 GEL per year). Work for sole traders or companies cannot use that status and is taxed at 20% of profit. Source: JUST Advisors, https://en.justadvisors.ge/stroitelstvo-remont-pereplanirovka-gruzii **[tax rules: verify with an accountant]**.

**Status: licence requirement for a marketplace's crews is UNVERIFIED.** This is the most important open legal question for the supply side.

## C. Consumer protection for construction work

**Law of Georgia on the Protection of Consumer Rights.** Official text: https://matsne.gov.ge/en/document/view/5420598 (English). Applies to a trader supplying goods or services to a consumer for private purposes. Mixed-purpose contracts are covered where private interests come first. Source of text read: matsne English page, relevant articles as quoted below.

| Article | What it says (paraphrase, from the matsne English text) | Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Art. 5 | Before a contract, the trader must give reliable and complete information in Georgian, clearly. | Contract and quote must be in Georgian (and offered in Russian/English as extras). |
| Art. 18(1)–(2) | A trader must deliver services as the contract specifies. If a time limit is missed or a defect appears, the consumer must give a reasonable extra period before further remedies. | Defines the default remedy chain for late or poor work. |
| Art. 22(1) | Unfair standard terms are void if they breach good faith and cause an unjustified imbalance against the consumer. | Platform terms and crew contract terms must be fair; a court can strike unfair clauses. |
| Art. 22 (ambiguity) | An ambiguous contract is read in the consumer's favour. | Write clear milestone and acceptance terms. |
| Art. 17 | Goods defects: repair, replacement, price reduction or withdrawal. | Applies to materials supplied by the crew. |
| Art. 9 | Fee rules: traders may not charge consumers extra for payment instruments above the trader's own cost; contact-number rules. | Relevant to any owner service fee and to card or payment-link charges. |

Also in the matsne text (not yet read in full): withdrawal rights for distance contracts (Arts. 12–13), and whether a platform itself is a "trader". **[unverified]** The JUST Advisors guide advises a written contract for any construction work, because verbal agreements do not protect the contractor.

**Open question:** whether the platform counts as a "trader" (and so bears Consumer Law duties) or only an intermediary. **[unverified; lawyer question]**

## D. Contract for work and payment (Civil Code of Georgia)

Civil Code of Georgia: https://matsne.gov.ge/en/document/view/31702

- **Contract for work** (Civil Code, Arts. 629 onward, per search summary): the contractor undertakes to perform the specified work and the client pays the agreed price. Payment is due after the work is done, unless the contract provides for instalments. **[unverified in full text; check Arts. 629–656]**
- **Advance payments:** the only advance we found in the construction context is a right of the client to request money in advance to fix a defect. We did not find a general rule either allowing or limiting advance payments for construction. **[unverified]**
- **Written contract:** the JUST Advisors guide recommends a written contract with scope, deadlines, price and payment procedure, acceptance, change procedure, termination and disputes. It also suggests a late-payment penalty (e.g. 0.1% per day) and an acceptance clause (deemed accepted after 5 working days without motivated comments). Those are the author's suggestions, not statutory terms. https://en.justadvisors.ge/stroitelstvo-remont-pereplanirovka-gruzii

**Note on a false lead:** a search returned Georgia's "Prompt Pay Act" (O.C.G.A. 13-11-4, 15-day payment rule). That is a **US state (Georgia, USA)** statute and does **not** apply in Tbilisi. Do not cite it.

## E. Escrow, deposits and staged payments

- **National Bank of Georgia (NBG), 12 January 2024:** under the instructions on opening accounts in banking institutions, **only a commercial bank can act as escrow agent** under an escrow agreement. The NBG invited non-bank institutions to submit escrow schemes to its regulatory sandbox (applications 15 Jan–1 Sep 2024), to shape a future framework. Source: https://nbg.gov.ge/en/media/news/invitation-to-participate-in-the-e-scrow-service-project-by-the-national-bank-of-georgia-s
- **Status after 2024: UNVERIFIED.** We did not find a later NBG rule or a sandbox outcome. Check with the NBG Innovation Office (innovationoffice@nbg.gov.ge, from the notice).
- **Practical reading:** a platform-held escrow in Georgia would, on current published rules, run through a commercial bank escrow account, or through a licensed partner, not through the platform's own account. Staged release by the platform without a bank would be a regulatory question.
- **Payment partners:** the NBG regulates payment service providers. **[unverified]** Check the current NBG list of licensed payment providers before naming a partner.
- **Market reference:** Tadilat.com (Turkey) advertises milestone payments (secondary source only). https://enests.co/blog/top-10-best-home-renovation-companies-in-turkey

**Implication for the money-flow decision:** introduction-only with manual stage payments is legally simpler. Platform-held escrow needs a bank partner or regulatory approval and is not yet a clear route.
