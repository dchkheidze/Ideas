# Competitor research: renovation-crew marketplace, Georgia (Tbilisi assumed)

Research date: 2026-11-20. Public sources only. Every fact has a link. Items marked **[unverified]** could not be confirmed from a primary or first-party source. Prices are as published by the named company or directory, not checked by us. Platform claims ("zero commission", "verified", "average response time") are the company's own statements.

Scope note: the idea is an owner-facing marketplace for whole-apartment renovation, with one brief, several bids, staged payments and a contract. Most Tbilisi players below sell small services or sell a firm's own crew. Few sell an owner-brief-to-bids flow for full renovation.

## 4. Positioning map and gap (for the founder-competitors and founder-marketing passes)

| Dimension | Renovation firms (RemontX, KPI, Gamiremonte) | Lead boards (xFix, Homico, ss.ge) | Directories (All Masters, Madloba) | Proposed idea |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Owner posts one brief for a whole flat | No | Partly (small jobs) | No | **Yes** |
| Several verified crews bid on the same brief | No (one firm quotes) | Yes, for small jobs | No | Yes |
| Owner gets help choosing and signing a contract | Firm provides its own contract | No | No | Yes |
| Money held and released in stages | No, not mentioned | No, pays pro directly | No | Proposed |
| Owner fee | Included in firm price | None (claimed) | None | Under test |

**Gap the idea can own, if validated:** whole-flat renovation, not small jobs, with a standard brief, a bid comparison, a written contract and staged payment. xFix and Homico already cover owner-posts-and-pros-bid for small jobs and the Turnkey category, and they charge no owner fee. So the idea must win on the contract and money-protection layer, not on the bidding flow. A pilot should test whether owners pay for the protection.
