# Board brief: builder marketplace v2 (Georgia)

Read `idea.md` in this folder. It describes a platform in Georgia (Tbilisi assumed) that connects private apartment owners with renovation crews. The owner posts one standard brief, verified crews bid, the platform helps choose and set up the contract, and money moves in stages after sign-off. Revenue is hybrid and undecided. The founder is a small team of friends and family with a tech or product background; the budget is $5k to $25k and the pilot is 2 to 3 months out.

Several items are not decided or not known: exact city, money-flow branch, revenue split, crew count, legal form, acquisition channels. Treat them as open questions, not facts. Do not assume a market size or competitor set.
