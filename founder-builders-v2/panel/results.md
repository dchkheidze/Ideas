- A written verification standard I can read, and a written promise of what happens if a crew stops mid-job, before I post a brief.
- A written verification standard for the crews, a clear stop-mid-job plan, and a few past Tbilisi clients I can call who were happy with the work and the schedule.
- A named verification process for the crews and a written promise on what happens if a crew stops mid-job, with my money protected.
- A written rule for a crew that stops mid-job, with the money held back until it is finished, and the crew checks spelled out before I see any names.
- A published verification process for the crews, a clear stop-mid-job guarantee, and reviews from real Tbilisi owners who used the service and finished on time.
- A written verification process for the crews and a clear answer on what happens if a crew stops mid-job, shown to me before I post my brief.

- A written, specific verification process I can read before I sign, plus a clear rule for a crew that stops mid-job, such as a named replacement crew or a money-back step.

- A written, visible verification process for the crews, plus a clear promise that if a crew stops mid-job I get my money back or a replacement crew at no extra cost.
