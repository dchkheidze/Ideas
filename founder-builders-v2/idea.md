# Builder marketplace v2 (working name: not chosen)

- What it is: an online platform that connects private owners who want an apartment renovated with renovation crews, and mediates the job: the owner posts one standard written brief, verified crews bid on it, the platform helps choose and set up the contract, and money moves in stages after sign-off. Revenue is hybrid (builder fee per won contract plus an optional customer service fee); the pricing pass will test each.
- Scope: flat (apartment) renovation first. New houses are a later phase, only after one city works.
- Market: Georgia. City assumed to be Tbilisi (not yet confirmed by the founder). Languages: Georgian, Russian, English.
- Who it is for: private owners of apartments in Tbilisi who renovate their own flat or a rental. Builders and renovation crews are the supply side.
- Team and founder: small startup with several people, mostly friends and family. Founder background is tech or product; trade knowledge and crew access must come from partners.
- Budget and timeline: $5k to $25k; first pilot in the next 2 to 3 months.
- Legal and payment: company not yet registered. A lawyer and a payment or escrow partner are affordable within budget.
- Builder relationships: 1 to 4 crews known informally; they would agree to work on a first stage informally, not yet on a written partner agreement.
- Money flow: undecided. Board to compare introduction-only (with manual stage payments) against platform-held escrow, with costs and legal requirements for Georgia.
- Revenue: undecided, hybrid acceptable. Pricing pass to test a builder fee per won contract and an owner service fee.

## Open items the board should treat as unknown
- Exact city, number of verified crews reachable in that city, Georgian escrow and payment rules, company form, and acquisition channels.
