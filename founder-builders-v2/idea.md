# Builder marketplace v2 (working name: not chosen)

- What it is: an online platform for apartment owners in Tbilisi, Georgia, who want a whole flat renovated. The owner posts one standard written brief; verified crews bid on it; the platform helps choose and sets up a written contract with a stage schedule and sign-off steps. The owner pays the crew directly, per stage, after sign-off.
- Base case decided (founder, 20 Nov 2026): revenue from the builder side only, per won contract or as a subscription (to be tested in the pricing pass). The owner pays no platform fee. Money flow: direct payment between owner and crew (introduction and contract only). Escrow through a bank partner is a later option, not part of the first pilot.
- Scope: flat (apartment) renovation first. New houses are a later phase.
- Market: Georgia. City assumed to be Tbilisi (not yet confirmed by the founder). Languages: Georgian, Russian, English.
- Competitors already live in Tbilisi: xFix and Homico (owner posts a job, pros bid, owner pays the pro directly, no owner commission). Gap: whole-flat renovation with one written contract, stage schedule and sign-off.
- Team: small startup, several people, mostly friends and family. Founder background: tech or product. Trade knowledge and crew access must come from partners.
- Budget and timeline: $5k to $25k; first pilot in 2 to 3 months.
- Legal and payment: company not yet registered. Lawyer available within budget.
- Builder relationships: 1 to 4 crews known informally, willing to work a first stage informally, no written partner agreement yet.

## Open items the board should treat as unknown
- Exact city, owner counts and renovation frequency in Tbilisi, crew count reachable for flat work, builder fee level, owner acquisition cost, legal form, licensing for interior work, and whether escrow is needed later.
