# Monopoly lens
Score: 3 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- The model has working references. The founder names pzzl.ru and stroim.dom.rf, so demand for this kind of platform exists, though the brief gives us no size for either.
- The model is two-sided and can compound. More builders attract more customers and the reverse, which is a real network effect if one local market reaches density.
- Mediating the contract and holding payment could become a trust layer that is hard to copy. The brief leaves escrow undecided, so this is a possibility, not a moat yet.
- Flat renovation is a different buyer from someone commissioning a new house. It could be the easier small market to own first. Its size is unknown.

## What would kill it (top 3, most dangerous first)
1. No small market is chosen. City, country and job type are all open, and the scope covers new houses and flat renovation at once. Two-sided liquidity needs density in one place. Spread across markets, both sides start thin and the network effect never forms.
2. Established platforms in the same space. The brief does not say how this idea differs from pzzl.ru or stroim.dom.rf for the same buyer. Without a reason a builder or customer picks this one, a copycat in month six is as easy as the original.
3. No distribution plan. Acquisition channels are open. A house or renovation job is a large, infrequent purchase, so winning each customer may be expensive. If the cost of the first 100 customers exceeds what a commission earns on one job, the model fails before the network forms.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Which city or country, and which one job type do you own first: new house or flat renovation?
- How do the first 100 customers arrive, and what does each one cost?
- How is this different from pzzl.ru and stroim.dom.rf for the buyer you pick? What have you checked about them?
- How many builders must you sign in that city before a customer can post a job?
- What is your secret: know-how, builder relationships or data that others lack?
- Why now, when the references already exist?
- What stops a copycat in month six?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. Name one city and one job type, and size that small market with a sourced number. The brief has no number.
2. State the wedge in one sentence: why a named buyer picks this over pzzl.ru or stroim.dom.rf. Write down the reference checks behind it.
3. Give a distribution plan for the first 100 customers with a cost per customer, and hand it to the CFO to test against a commission.
4. Sign a set number of builders in the chosen city before any customer is promised bids. The founder sets the number.
5. Re-score after these are answered. Until then the score stays at 3.
