# Board brief: builder marketplace

Read `idea.md` in this folder. It describes a platform that connects private customers wanting a house built or an apartment renovated with home-builder and renovation companies, and mediates the deal (job description, builder bids, contract, payment). Reference models named by the founder: pzzl.ru and stroim.dom.rf. Scope includes flat renovation as well as new builds.

Several items are not yet decided (name, city/country, price or commission, acquisition channels, payment model). Treat them as open questions, not as facts. Do not assume a market size, a competitor set or a budget.

Your job: apply ONLY the lens you were given to this idea, score it, name the risks that would kill it, and vote FUND / FUND IF / PASS.
