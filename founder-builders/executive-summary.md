## What it is

An online platform where private owners who want a flat renovation (first) or a house built describe the job once in a standard brief, verified builders bid on it, and the platform helps select and contract them. Not yet decided: city, country, fee, who pays it, and whether the platform holds money in escrow or only introduces the parties.

## The three numbers behind the verdict

These use the base-case placeholders: a 3% take on a 350,000 contract, 1,000 per brief, and 5% of briefs becoming contracts. Currency is not decided, so the units are unlabelled.

- **Margin:** each signed job loses 9,500 before fixed costs (-471% margin at plan).
- **Break-even:** never reached in the base case. Positive cases exist only if the fee rises (a flat 25,000) or the brief-to-contract rate rises to 10%, and they still need a sourced market size.
- **Year 1:** an operating loss of about 5.9 million on 10 signed jobs a month. Cash needed before it pays for itself: about 6.4 million.

## What the panel and the board agreed on

- **Buyers:** 0 of 20 simulated buyers would buy. 16 of 20 passed on trust, 3 on the unstated fee, 1 on habit. The fix they asked for is the same each time: verified builders with checkable references, a stated fee, and payment held until each stage is signed off.
- **Board:** 3 of 3 "fund if", average score 3.7 of 10. Conditions: one city, one job type (flat renovation is the candidate), decide escrow or introduction-only, and verify builders before listing.
- **Where they differ:** the board's strength-of-case views vary, and it is unclear whether renovation or new builds should come first. The two reference platforms (pzzl.ru, stroim.dom.rf) are new-house products, not renovation.

## The biggest risk and what will be done about it

The money seam. Escrow means milestones, disputes and refunds, a much larger build, and a legal question the sources do not settle for flat renovation. Introduction-only lets customer and builder sign off-platform and skip the fee. Until a lawyer confirms the rules, no public material may claim money is "held" or "protected".
