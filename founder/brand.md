**Shortlist:** ① **Dabrundi**, ② **Kidev**, ③ **HoReCa Hub** (keep as the descriptive B2B line, e.g. "Dabrundi for Business").

**Recommendation: Dabrundi.** It *is* the promise ("come back"), says it in the owners' own language, and no business in this category came up. Use the descriptive line with it: **"Dabrundi: guest payments, feedback and loyalty for restaurants."**

## 2. Promise, tagline, voice

**The promise:** *Every guest who pays leaves you something useful: honest feedback that comes to you first, and a reason to come back.*

**Tagline options:**
1. **"Pay. Rate. Come back."** / "გადაიხადე. შეაფასე. დაბრუნდი." *(recommended: it explains the loop and ends on the name)*
2. "Every bill brings them back."
3. "Know who comes back."

**Voice: three adjectives**

| Adjective | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm** (Georgian hospitality) | Talk like a good host: "your guests", "your table" | Corporate words: "solutions", "leverage", "ecosystem" |
| **Straight** (trust is the #1 blocker) | Put the fee, the payout time and the contract terms in plain numbers | Hide costs in "fees may apply" or asterisks |
| **Practical** (owners are short on time) | One clear action per message; talk in waiter minutes saved | Feature lists or tech jargon ("CRM", "API") |

**Before / after (a line from `pitch.md`):**
- **Before:** "Guest Loop plan, 39 GEL a month: dish ratings from guests who paid come to you privately first, and you choose what goes public."
- **After:** "For 39 GEL a month, you hear from every paying guest first, and you decide what the world sees."

## 3. The look (brief)

**Colour** (contrast checked against WCAG; AA needs 4.5:1 for normal text):

| Colour | Hex | Job | Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Saperavi** (deep wine red) | `#7A1F3D` | Primary: logo, buttons, headers. Warm and Georgian, and far from QRMENUS's blue | White on it 10.0:1 ✅; on cream 9.3:1 ✅ |
| **Cream** | `#FBF6EE` | Background: paper menus, table stands, app | Ink on it 15.9:1 ✅ |
| **Ink** | `#1E1B18` | Body text | 15.9:1 on cream ✅ |
| **Leaf** (accent) | `#2F7D5B` | "Paid / came back" success states only | White on it 5.0:1 ✅; on cream 4.6:1 ✅ (just passes; use it for large or bold text). **Don't** put ink text on leaf (3.4:1 ❌) |

**Type** (both free under the SIL Open Font License, and both **support Georgian script**, which is a must):
- **Display:** *Noto Serif Georgian* (Google Fonts) for headings, the logo wordmark and table stands.
- **Text:** *FiraGO* (bBox Type, on GitHub) for the app, the website and receipts. It covers Georgian, Latin and Cyrillic, so one font handles all three languages.

**Logo brief:**
- **It must say** return, hospitality and trust: for example a loop or arrow that turns back into a table or plate, or the Georgian letter დ (the first letter of დაბრუნდი) shaped as a returning path.
- **It must work:**
  - on a 10×10 cm table stand next to a QR code
  - as a 32px app icon
  - in one colour on a receipt
  - on the dark wine red and on cream
- **Avoid:**
  - QR-code imagery as the logo (every competitor does it)
  - forks and knives (generic)
  - the vine cross and other heritage symbols (overused, and sensitive)
  - anything that looks like Wox, SQR, QRMENUS or Keepz marks
- An image tool can generate **concepts** if you'd like. They would be concepts for a designer to refine, not a finished, registrable logo.

## 4. The first five touchpoints

1. **The table stand** (what diners see first): cream card, wine-red header *"Pay here, no app needed"*, the QR, then a small line: *"Your tip goes to your waiter · Your rating goes to the owner first."* In Georgian on the front, with English and Russian lines below.
2. **The owner's landing page** (founding sign-up): headline *"Every paid bill can become a Google review."*, then three facts: free QR payment, the Keepz fee printed in GEL, a 39 GEL plan (29 for founding restaurants). The guarantee box and the two contract clauses (private ratings, the guest list belongs to the restaurant) appear in plain language above the sign-up button.
3. **The payment confirmation the diner sees:** *"Paid ✓ Thank you! How was the khinkali?"* with one-tap ratings per dish, then *"Loved it? Tell Google too →"*. No forced sign-up.
4. **The first Instagram post:** *"Built in Tbilisi, for Tbilisi tables."* Show a real photo of a pilot table stand (with the venue's permission) and one sentence on why: tourists' reviews and tips should help the restaurant.
5. **Reply to the first complaint** (template): *"Thank you for telling us, [name]. We've [what we did] and [what changes so it doesn't happen again]. If it cost you anything, here's what we'll do: [concrete fix]. My number is [phone] if you'd like to talk. [Founder name]"*. Name the cause, the fix and a person; no "we apologise for any inconvenience".

## Notes

- Never copy a competitor's name, logo, colours or tagline.
- Use only fonts, images and icons you have a licence for (the two fonts above are free to use commercially).
- This isn't legal advice on trademarks. Register the name, the domains and the handles before printing anything.
