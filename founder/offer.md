# Offer: "Founding Restaurants", HoReCa Hub Guest Loop

Method: the Offers lens (a summary of the framework in *$100M Offers*, in our own words). Inputs: `panel/results.md` (100 owners), `competitors.md`, `pricing.md`, `cfo.md`. All amounts are in GEL.

## 1. The problem list (in the owners' words, from the panel)

**Before buying**
1. "Who is the payment company? What's the fee, and when do I get the money?" (the most common blocker)
2. "One bad khinkali night stays public forever." (fear of public dish ratings)
3. "Will this startup still exist in a year, and who owns my guest list?"
4. "59 lari plus another transaction fee on top." (two fees stacked)
5. "No proof it pays back. Show me a café like mine."
6. "My tourists review on Google and Tripadvisor, not in your app."
7. "I already pay for Wox/SQR and a card terminal."
8. "Loyalty programs are for chains; I know my regulars."
9. "Instagram and WhatsApp already do my marketing for free."

**During setup and use**
10. "My waiters will fight anything that touches tips."
11. "I'm two waiters short and have no time to train anyone."
12. "Two QR codes on the table will confuse guests." (the menu QR plus the payment QR)
13. "My manager will have to match QR payments against the POS every night."
14. "My older regulars want to hand over cash or a card."
15. "My guests order at the counter, not at the table."
16. "Split bills: part QR, part terminal."

**After**
17. "What if hardly anyone pays by QR? Then I'm paying for nothing."
18. "If the payout is slow, I can't pay suppliers that week."
19. "If the platform shuts down, I lose my reviews and guest data."
20. "Could the diner app send my regulars to competitors?"

## 3. The stack

- **Core: "Know who comes back, and bring them back."** QR payment with no app for guests, private verified dish feedback, guest visit history, a ready-made loyalty reward and messages to your own guests. **39 GEL a month.**
- **Bonus 1: free QR payment and listings,** which answers "two fees stacked" (problem 4).
- **Bonus 2: done-for-you setup and staff training,** which answers "no time, short-staffed" (problem 11).
- **Bonus 3: Google/Tripadvisor review prompt,** which answers "my tourists review elsewhere" (problem 6).
- **Risk reversal:** private ratings by default, the guest list owned by the restaurant in the contract, and tips straight to waiters (problems 2, 3, 10).
- **Guarantee:** if fewer than 1 in 5 bills are paid by QR in the first paid month, that month is free.
- **Real scarcity:** first 30 restaurants or 31 March 2027, whichever comes first. 3 months free, then 29 GEL locked for 12 months, in exchange for a case study.
- **Name:** *Founding Restaurants*.

**What the guarantee costs:** at most one month's fee per restaurant (29–39 GEL; the CFO's blended price is 37). If 30% of 150 paying restaurants claimed it in year 1, that's 45 × 37 ≈ **1,665 GEL** once. That's affordable next to a contribution of 31.25 GEL per restaurant a month, every month (`cfo.md`). It also pushes us to make sure guests actually use QR, which the business needs anyway.

**Before this goes live:** naming Keepz needs a signed agreement with Keepz, and the tip and payout details must match its real terms. Have a lawyer check the guarantee and contract wording against Georgian consumer and advertising rules. This isn't legal advice.

## 5. Re-test: the same 20 owners (same cards, seed 149257)

| | v1 pitch (59 GEL) | Founding offer (39 / 29 GEL) |
|---|:-:|:-:|
| Would sign up | **6 / 20 (30%)** | **7 / 20 (35%)** |
| Passed for **trust** | 6 | **3** |
| Passed for need / habit / timing / convenience / price | 3 / 3 / 1 / 1 / 0 | 3 / 2 / 3 / 1 / 1 |
| Median price answers (too cheap / bargain / expensive / too expensive) | 10 / 30 / 70 / 110 | 5 / 22.5 / 49.5 / 79.5 |

- **One owner changed from no to yes:** P002 (tourist venue). Private ratings and the Google prompt answered her fear of public ratings. Her remaining condition: *"if the contract does not say in writing that no rating goes public without her approval."*
- **Trust objections halved.** Naming the partner, private ratings and data ownership worked.
- **What still blocks** (the offer can't fix these alone):
  1. **The fee still isn't printed.** *"the exact Keepz fee written in the offer itself, the same as or below her terminal, with next-day payout"* (P013); P018 said the same. You need the real Keepz quote.
  2. **No POS link.** *"'nothing changes in your POS' reads to her as 'not connected to my POS'… staff would have to match two systems every night"* (P004); P010 said the same.
  3. **Wrong time or format:** owners who are short-staffed right now (P006, P014, P016) and counter-service cafés (P005, P015).
- **Price answers fell** (the "getting expensive" median went from 70 to 49.5). The owners probably anchored on the 29/39 GEL prices shown in the pitch. 39 GEL still sits below the new "getting expensive" median.

**Verdict:** the offer helps (trust halved, +1 buyer), but this is a small sample and these are simulated owners leaning agreeable. What's left is **the market fit for cafés with counter service and the missing POS link**, not the offer wording. **Next moves:**
1. Get the Keepz fee and payout terms and print them in the offer.
2. Target table-service restaurants, early adopters and upscale or tourist venues first.
3. Treat the Wox/SQR link as the main product priority after the MVP.
