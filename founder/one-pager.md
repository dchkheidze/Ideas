# Dabrundi (working name: HoReCa Hub) · One page

**Verdict: Not yet.** Year 1 makes an operating loss on the current plan, and 22% of simulated owners sign up against a 25% bar. What would change that is below.

**The problem.** Independent Tbilisi restaurants can't tell which guests come back, and public review sites accept anyone's rating. Card payments, loyalty stamp cards and reviews are separate tools that aren't connected.

**The customer.** Owners of independent table-service restaurants in Tbilisi. They start with upscale and tourist-area venues and owners who like new tools (these signed up at 40% and 100% on the simulated panel).

**The offer.**
- Guests pay at the table by QR code, with no app, through a licensed Georgian payment partner.
- Every payment becomes a private, verified dish rating for the owner, a guest profile (first visit or regular) and a loyalty reward.
- Happy guests are invited to review on Google or Tripadvisor.
- Free listing and HoReCa boards for suppliers, jobs and premises.
- **Guest Loop 39 GEL/month**, Pro 99 GEL/month.
- **Founding Restaurants** (first 30, until 31 March 2027): 3 months free, then 29 GEL locked for 12 months.
- Guarantee: the first paid month is free if fewer than 1 in 5 bills are paid by QR.

The lean plan, a CFO what-if where the founder does all the sales and spends 750 GEL a month on ads: break-even **43**, year 1 **+15,782**, cash needed **~11,500**.

**The evidence.**
- **Competitor gap:** no Tbilisi tool connects payment, verified reviews and repeat guests. Keepz does payment, Wox does loyalty, Rezto does booking, and Tripadvisor and Google host unverified reviews (`competitors.md`).
- **Simulated panel:** 100 owners, 22% sign up, mostly for the free trial. The top barriers were trust (34), habit (25) and need (13). The rebuilt offer took the same 20 owners from 30% to 35% and halved the trust objections.
- **Real test:** none yet. A deposit pre-sign-up runs 14 Dec 2026 – 3 Jan 2027 (target: 10 deposits from 60 owners).

**What the money is for.** About 11,500–22,600 GEL to reach break-even:
- Company setup and contracts
- Lovable and Supabase
- QR table stands for 30 founding venues
- Launch marketing and owner-targeted ads
- Optionally, a part-time sales helper

**The ask.**
- A signed **payment partner** (Keepz, Bank of Georgia or TBC) with the fee per payment, payout time, tips and a revenue share in writing.
- **3 friendly table-service restaurants** for the January pilot.
- **Funding:** about 11,500 GEL for the lean plan.

_The panel is simulated buyers and the numbers are projections. Real customers and real quotes confirm them. Not financial, legal or tax advice._
