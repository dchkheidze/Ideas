## What would flip a no

- If it clearly brought me new customers I don't have now, with the free months showing real new faces, and the payment fee was lower than what my bank terminal charges.
- If dish ratings came to me privately first, so I could fix the problem before anything goes public, and the guest payment fee was no higher than my current terminal.
- If the dish ratings came only to me privately, with nothing public unless I chose to show it, and it plugged into my existing POS so my staff don't have a second system to learn.
- Proof from a café my size in Tbilisi that guests really pay by QR and that the offers bring back enough extra visits to cover the 59 lari and the fees, plus a per-transaction fee no higher than my current terminal.
- Proof from another wine bar in Sololaki or Vake that tips went up or stayed the same with the QR payment, plus the exact per-transaction fee written down before I sign.
- The partner named, a written fee lower than my current terminal fee, and money in my account the next day. Then I'd try the 3 free months and keep it only if it brings back regulars worth more than 59 lari in the first month after.
- A signed contract that the guest list is mine and exportable any time, plus two or three respected venues in Sololaki or Vake running it for six months without problems.
- If it plugged straight into my Wox/SQR so there's no second system, and the payment fee was no higher than my terminal's, and other owners I know showed me it brought real repeat visits or tourist bookings.
- A direct integration with Wox/SQR so the bill closes in my POS automatically, plus proof from a few Tbilisi restaurants that tourists actually leave reviews and come back through it.
- Name a bank I know as the payment partner, show a per-transaction fee no higher than my current terminal's, and pay out next day. Then I'd use the 3 free months on the summer terrace to see if tourists actually pay and review through the QR.
- If the staff board actually got me two decent waiters fast, and another Tbilisi restaurant showed me the QR payments really cut table turnover time and brought regulars back, I'd try the free months.
- If the per-transaction fee was lower than what my bank terminal charges, so switching guests to QR payment actually saved me money instead of adding a cost.
