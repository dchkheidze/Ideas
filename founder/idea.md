# HoReCa Hub (working name)

- What it is: a two-sided HoReCa ecosystem — a B2B platform connecting restaurants with suppliers, staff and premises, plus a free B2C diner app for discovery, booking, QR payment and verified reviews that feeds restaurants customer data, loyalty and marketing tools.
- B2B scope: a listings marketplace only (suppliers, jobs, properties); it connects the parties and does not run the deals. Decided: B2B listings are a free hook that brings restaurants onto the platform; revenue comes from the QR payment, guest data and loyalty product.
- Who it is for: first, independent restaurants in Georgia (B2B side: restaurant owners and their suppliers of goods and services); diners in Georgia (B2C side).
- What it sells, at what price: tiered subscriptions for restaurant owners and suppliers starting from 100 GEL/month, with a free tier with limited functions. During the MVP stage everything is free. A fee on QR payments is under evaluation and may be bundled into subscription tiers. The diner app is free.
- Where and how: Georgia (starting city not yet specified). Delivered as web/mobile apps. Customer acquisition channels not yet specified.
- Budget and constraints: not yet specified (budget, team, technical skills, timeline and existing industry relationships are open).
