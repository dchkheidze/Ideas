# Marketing: HoReCa Hub, Founding Restaurants (Tbilisi)

Audience for this plan: **restaurant owners, the paying side.** Diner marketing starts once 30 or more restaurants take QR payments; before that, a diner app has nothing to show.

Evidence: `panel/results.md` (100 simulated owners), `offer.md` (re-test on 20), `competitors.md`, `pricing.md`, `cfo.md`. All amounts are in GEL.

## 1. Who first

The panel's sign-up rates (simulated, so treat them as an upper bound):

| Group | Sign-up rate |
|---|---:|
| Early tech adopters | **100%** (15 of 15) |
| Upscale / tourist-centre venues (Old Tbilisi, Sololaki, Vake) | **40%** |
| Small cafés, mid-size restaurants | 18% |
| Already on Wox/SQR | 5% |
| Instagram-first owners, "I know my regulars" owners | 0% |

**First audience: owners of table-service, upscale or tourist-area venues in Old Tbilisi, Sololaki, Vake and Vera who like new tools.** Counter-service cafés come later (QR at the table doesn't fit them, P005 and P015), and so do venues that are deep in Wox until the POS link exists.

## 2. Positioning

**A.** For tourist-area restaurant owners in Tbilisi who *"live on Tripadvisor and Google stars"*, HoReCa Hub is the table QR payment that turns every paid bill into private dish feedback and more Google reviews, unlike Tripadvisor, where anyone can post anything.

**B.** For independent Tbilisi restaurants that *"know their regulars by name"* but not the other 80% of guests, HoReCa Hub is the free QR payment that shows you who's new and who came back, and gives them a reason to return, unlike stamp-card loyalty that a waiter has to remember.

**C.** For short-staffed restaurant owners, HoReCa Hub is free pay-at-the-table that saves your waiters trips with the terminal, plus guest data that's yours, unlike adding another POS.

**Recommended: A for the first 30 restaurants.**
- It matches the group with the highest sign-up rate (upscale and tourist venues, 40%).
- It's the gap no one fills: Tripadvisor and Google reviews are unverified, and no Tbilisi tool links payment to reviews (`competitors.md` §5).
- It answers the objection that turned P002 from no to yes (private ratings plus a Google prompt).
- Use **C** as the opening line with short-staffed owners. It came up often (*"I'm two waiters short"*), and P003, P033 and P061 bought for it.
- Keep **B** for later. The panel's "regulars" owners didn't respond to it at all (0%).

**What not to sound like:** Wox ("Pay only for what you use", a software price list) or SQR ("QR menu and POS in one system", features). Talk about the result, more stars and guests who return, not about modules.

## 3. Channels

| Channel | Why it fits this buyer | Cost | How you'll know it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Founder visits in person**, 15:00–17:30, the quiet hours between lunch and dinner, district by district (Sololaki → Old Tbilisi → Vera → Vake) | Trust was the #1 blocker (34 owners). Owners buy from a face, and setup is done in person anyway. The panel kept asking for *"proof from a venue nearby"*, and walking the neighbourhood builds that | Your time; printed one-page leaflets ~150 GEL | Visits → demos → signed founding sign-ups. Target: 1 in 5 visits leads to a demo, 1 in 3 demos signs |
| **2. Meta ads aimed at owners** (Facebook + Instagram, Tbilisi, interests: restaurant owner / hospitality / food service; Georgian and English) driving to a 1-page founding sign-up | Owners already live on Instagram and Facebook (the panel's "Instagram-first" group). Cheap way to fill the visit calendar | **750 GEL a month** (CFO lean plan) | Cost per demo booked; target under 60 GEL |
| **3. Partners who already visit restaurants:** Keepz (co-marketing to its merchants), wholesalers and suppliers (they get a free spot on the supplier board in exchange for introductions), and a HoReCa association or chef community if one will partner | Borrowed trust plus distribution; the B2B boards give suppliers a reason to help | ~0 GEL (free listings, a shared case study) | Introductions per partner per month; target 5+ |

**Not doing now:**
- **Diner ads, TikTok and food creators:** no point until 30+ restaurants take QR payments.
- **Google Ads:** owners don't search for "QR payment"; they don't know it exists.
- **Cold calls:** the trust problem gets worse on the phone.
- **Big launch PR:** wait until there are real case studies to show.

## 6. Budget and the most you can pay for a customer

**Budget (CFO lean plan):**
- **Before launch:** launch marketing 2,500 GEL + QR table stands for 30 founding venues 2,700 GEL (`numbers.json` startup).
- **Every month:** **750 GEL** Meta ads aimed at owners; leaflets ~150 GEL. Founder visits cost your time. Partners cost ~0.

**The most you can pay to win one paying restaurant:**
- Each paying restaurant leaves **31.25 GEL a month** (`cfo.md`).
- Rule: earn back what you spent to win it within 6 paying months, so **31.25 × 6 = 187.50 GEL per paying restaurant.**
- The panel says each owner who says yes signs up for **1** trial in the first month, and most of those yeses are trial-only. If **1 in 2 trials becomes a paying restaurant** (an assumption to check in the pilot), the most you can pay per trial is 187.50 ÷ 2 ≈ **94 GEL**.
- At 750 GEL a month in ads, that means getting at least **8 trials a month** from paid channels (750 ÷ 94).

## 7. Three numbers to watch every week

| Number | Target | Change something if… |
|---|---|---|
| Founding sign-ups per week (all channels) | 4–5 a week, to reach 30 by 31 March | Below 2 a week for 2 weeks in a row: change the district, the hook or the demo |
| QR share of bills at live venues | ≥ 1 in 5 (the guarantee level) | Below 1 in 10 at a venue after 2 weeks: retrain staff, move the QR stand, check the tip flow |
| Ad cost per demo booked | ≤ 60 GEL | Above 94 GEL (the max per trial): pause ads, put the money into partners and visits |

## Rules this plan follows

- No fake reviews, testimonials, follower counts or "as seen on". Case-study numbers are published only when they're real and the owner has approved them.
- The scarcity is real: 30 spots and a fixed date. Show the honest count of spots left.
- Don't name Keepz in public ads until there's a signed agreement. Disclose any paid or co-marketing partnership.
- Check the guarantee and contract wording against Georgian advertising and consumer rules with a lawyer. This isn't legal advice.
