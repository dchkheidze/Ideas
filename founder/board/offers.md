# Offers lens
Score: 3/10 | Vote: PASS

## What is strong
- **The buyers are easy to find.** Independent restaurants have street addresses, sit close together and can be visited in person. Of the four market tests, reachability is the one this business passes clearly.
- **There is one strong outcome inside the list.** "Know who walked in, whether they came back, and bring them back again", with loyalty and campaigns run for the owner, is an outcome you can sell. Reviews tied to a real order and rated dish by dish could be the proof that makes it believable.
- **QR bill payment brings in data as a side effect.** If diners pay this way, the customer profile and the verified reviews fill up without anyone doing extra work.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **This is a list of features, not an offer.** Sourcing, hiring, property listings, a directory, booking, payment, reviews, loyalty and campaigns are about nine products for at least four kinds of buyer (restaurants, suppliers, job seekers, diners). There is no single result the owner pays for. The offer leads with "more modules", and "free during the MVP, then from 100 GEL" is competing on price. Nothing here makes saying no feel stupid.
2. **Likelihood is close to zero on day one.** The valuable part (diner profiles, repeat-visit data, loyalty, campaigns) only works once many diners use the app, and the diner app only matters once many restaurants are on it. The owner is asked to pay for a result that depends on a network that does not exist yet. There is no proof, no case study and no guarantee.
3. **The buyer's market is unproven on pain and money.** The pitch never shows that independent Georgian restaurants feel this pain badly enough, or have spare money to spend on fixing it. We do not know the willingness to pay, the number of restaurants or whether the market is growing. The effort is also heavy: staff have to change how the bill is settled and the owner has to run campaigns, in a sector short on time.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- If an owner could buy only one result from you, which is it, and what is it worth in GEL a month to them? (/founder-consumer)
- How many independent restaurants are in the starting city, and which city is it? (/founder-competitors)
- What do owners use today for booking, payment, loyalty and hiring, and what do those tools cost? (/founder-competitors)
- How does the restaurant get value in week one, before a single diner has downloaded the app?
- What guarantee could you afford, for example "X repeat visits in 90 days or the month is free"? What do QR payment processing and the regulations around it cost? (/founder-cfo)
- What does it cost to win one restaurant and one diner, and do those costs pay back on a 100 GEL subscription? (/founder-cfo)
- Could you charge double for the one module that matters and still be the obvious choice?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
Not applicable, because the vote is PASS. The vote would move to FUND IF if the founder:
- Cuts the idea to one buyer (the independent restaurant owner) and one outcome (repeat diners), sold on its own with QR payment as the way the data gets in. Sourcing, hiring and property get parked.
- Shows that the outcome is delivered before network effects kick in, using the restaurant's own diners.
- Gets paid pre-commitments from a set of restaurants at a real price, not a free MVP. The size of that set should come from the /founder-cfo break-even.
- Attaches a guarantee the CFO says the business can afford.
