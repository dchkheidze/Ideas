# Board brief: HoReCa Hub (working name)

This brief is self-contained. It describes the idea as the founder pitched it. Items marked OPEN have not been provided by the founder; treat them as open questions, do not assume values.

## The idea in one line
A two-sided HoReCa (hotels, restaurants, cafés) ecosystem in Georgia: a B2B platform for restaurant operators and a free consumer app for diners, linked by shared data.

## B2B side (paying side)
- Sourcing: restaurants find and contact potential suppliers of goods (food, beverages, equipment) and services.
- HR module: restaurants post jobs; job seekers find hospitality jobs.
- Property module: HoReCa properties listed for lease and for sale.
- Monetization: subscriptions paid by suppliers and by restaurant owners.

## B2C side (free for diners)
- Directory of all restaurants with rankings.
- Search, table booking, and payment of the bill by QR code.
- Reviews only allowed when tied to a real order, and rated at the level of each dish.
- From this data a customer profile is built, so a restaurant knows who is visiting and whether it is a first or a repeat visit.
- Restaurants can run their own loyalty program inside the platform without building anything themselves.
- Restaurants can run marketing campaigns and promote events to diners.

## Market and customer
- Country: Georgia. Starting city: OPEN.
- First target customer: independent restaurants (not chains, not hotels).
- Diners: Georgian consumers and visitors (no further segmentation provided).

## Pricing
- Tiered subscriptions, starting from 100 GEL per month; tiers unlock different functions.
- A free tier with limited functions exists.
- MVP stage: the whole platform is free.
- QR payment fee: under evaluation; may be a separate fee or included in a subscription tier.
- Consumer app: free.
